Frequently asked questions

Will the layout stay exactly the same?

For simple single-column documents the result is usually very close. Complex multi-column layouts, tables and formulas may shift and need manual touch-ups — PDF to Word is a re-layout, not a perfect copy.

Do scanned PDFs work?

Not yet. Scanned pages are images without selectable text, so they need OCR first. A text-based PDF is required for now.

Is there a file size limit?

Server conversion accepts files up to 50 MB. Browser conversion depends on your device memory.

Are my files kept on the server?

No. Uploaded PDFs and converted files are deleted automatically after your download finishes.

Which languages does the interface support?

English, 简体中文， Español, Português, Français and Deutsch. Use the language menu at the top right.
